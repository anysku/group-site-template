## Do I need an account to buy?

You need a free anySKU account to order ahead. You can browse every sale without one.

## Do I need bank details to sell?

Not to start. You can list items and make sales first, then set up payouts when you have earnings waiting.

## When is my card charged?

When you collect your order on the day. Until then the amount is only held.

## How does the organization earn from a sale?

Each sale has an agreed share. When an item sells, that share of the price goes to the organization holding the sale, and the rest goes to the seller.

## Where can I get help with the app?

See [anySKU's help pages](https://www.anysku.com/how-it-works), or [contact us](/contact/) about anything to do with our sales.
