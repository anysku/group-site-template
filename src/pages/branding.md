Use these to promote your organization's sale in bulletins, newsletters and on social media.

## Our logo and colors

Add your logo files to `public/branding/` and link them here.

## Suggested wording

> Our sale is on [date] at [place]. Browse the items and order ahead at [link to your sale]. Pay when you collect.

## Flyers and QR codes

Every sale has a printable flyer with a QR code that opens the sale. Find the flyer under each sale on the [Sales](/events/) page.

## Mentioning anySKU

You can say your sales take place "on anySKU" and link to them. For any other use of anySKU's name or logo, ask anySKU first.
